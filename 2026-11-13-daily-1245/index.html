The SEC chair backs the FASB’s new early-warning project

SEC Chairman Paul Atkins used yesterday’s financial reporting conference to praise the FASB’s new Current Trends and Emerging Issues project, which watches data infrastructure spending and non-traditional lending and has already added a private credit disclosure project. He also noted this month’s SEC approval of the PCAOB’s QC 1000 amendments.

Comfort letters may lose the 135-day rule

The PCAOB proposes replacing the 135-day convention that caps negative assurance in auditor comfort letters with a period running to the issuer’s next filing deadline. The FASB, the same week, told staff to draft final pension guidance for cash balance plans and took up goodwill impairment testing.
