Logo lockups

Logo lockups — pick one, or mix parts. Nothing here is live.

Every variant is the full nameplate — The Board on the left, The Study on the right, taglines under each — shown twice: once on The Board’s light ground, once on The Study’s dark ground. The Study half is the link in every variant: hover or tap it and it comes up to full strength with the accent underline, the same click treatment as the live plate. A–E are set in Playfair Display; only the geometry changes. G, at the top, is the combination — E’s composition carrying F’s letters. F is a rebuild of the treatment in the screenshot Josh sent — the 9:52 PM version, without the border.

G · The combination

The mix Josh asked for: E’s composition — a small “THE” with a short line flanking it on each side, the big word beneath, tagline under that — with F’s letters inside it. “THE” is plain upright roman caps, no blackletter, no script. The big words carry F’s treatment in new colors: Board in a pronounced red, Study in royal blue, the dark tracery left inside the strokes. No border around either lockup. Each publication keeps its own color in its chrome, too — a half’s underline sweep matches its word, red for The Board, blue for The Study, on both grounds.
