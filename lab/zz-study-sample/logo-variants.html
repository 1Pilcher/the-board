Blackletter wordmark — candidates

One-line blackletter marks, plain black ink, tagline beneath — the New York Times nameplate treatment. Each candidate is shown as the split pair at desktop masthead size and at phone size. Candidates D and E were added Nov 21, 2026 as easier-to-read candidates, at Josh’s request. Candidate F is the exception: color traced through the middle of the strokes, the way Josh described it. Its live frames use an SVG filter, so exact Chromium renders are set underneath them — what the renders show is what it looks like.
