Fifteen treatments for the mark beside a section head. Each one runs on its own clock, whether you scroll or not. The tile under each is identical, so the only thing being judged is the mark. The second five lean on texture, the shade lines an engraver cuts by hand. The last five drop the line entirely: a small working device opens the section instead, standing at the left of the section name and exactly as tall as the name itself.
