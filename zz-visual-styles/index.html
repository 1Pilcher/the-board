The Board · Chart Standard

One way to draw the numbers

The contest is over. What survives is one system: The Economist's discipline, with every numeral but the axis ticks struck out, every shape closed in the same fine border, and one cast of light across all four charts. This page is where the system gets standardized, down to the last number.

By the Rules, Numberless

The Economist's rules, kept whole: red reserved for the series carrying each chart's message, headline titles that state the insight in words alone, gray academic subtitles at half the headline's size, labels on the categories and nowhere else. Added to finish it: a subtle border on every shape and every panel, and one soft shadow, identical on everything.

Every role in the system, one value, measured off the charts above. This is the first draft of the law: when a chart is built for the paper, it is built to these numbers or it does not ship.
